Notes

  • Deep link: the tap should open the matching in-app screen (SR-1 daily report / the specific SR-3 / SR-4 ticket), where the full detail is shown.
  • Length: keep title under ~40 chars and body under ~90 so nothing truncates on the collapsed lock-screen view.
  • Personalization (later): the safest hook-preserving token is a name greeting in SR-1 only, e.g. {{first_name}} जी, आज के काम तैयार हैं — still no numbers revealed.
